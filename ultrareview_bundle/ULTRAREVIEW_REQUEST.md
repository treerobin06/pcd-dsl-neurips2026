# Requested ultrareview

Please perform a strict NeurIPS-style review of the full current paper and submission package.

Focus on the issues most likely to affect acceptance:

1. Does the title/abstract/intro tell a strong and coherent story?
2. Are the main contributions clear and defensible?
3. Are any claims over-strong, especially around "verified", "exact", "compile once", "held-out", "generalization", and "cost"?
4. Are the baselines fair enough for a NeurIPS systems/methods paper?
5. Are the QUITE, bnlearn, NB/HMM, mixed E2E, and cost results framed correctly?
6. Are any tables/figures confusing, redundant, or likely to trigger reviewer skepticism?
7. Are there citation gaps or suspicious bibliography entries?
8. Are there anonymity, checklist, reproducibility, or desk-reject risks?
9. What are the top P0/P1 changes before the May 6 full-paper deadline?

Please prioritize actionable findings over general praise.
For each serious issue, give:

- Severity.
- Exact file and section/line if possible.
- Why a reviewer would care.
- The smallest safe fix that preserves contribution strength.
